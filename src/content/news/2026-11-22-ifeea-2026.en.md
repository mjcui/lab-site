Chen Kexian attended the 13th International Forum on Electrical Engineering and Automation (IFEEA 2026) in Wuhan, Hubei Province from November 20 to 22, 2026, and presented a poster on behalf of the research group for the paper "Delay-Aware Distributed Optimization for Multi-Park Integrated Energy Systems".

Addressing the challenges that distributed optimization faces in multi-park integrated energy systems (CIESS)—namely communication latency, topology changes, and boundary coupling—the paper proposes a delay-aware distributed optimization method. It constructs a cloud-edge collaborative architecture comprising energy parks, consumption parks, and commercial parks, and introduces a delay-aware distributed optimization mechanism together with a boundary-coupling compensation strategy. Case studies verify the performance of the proposed method in terms of iterative convergence, computational efficiency, and communication overhead, providing theoretical support and operational references for distributed optimization of multi-park integrated energy systems.
