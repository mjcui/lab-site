Song Zhenhuan attended the 8th International Conference on Energy, Power and Grid (ICEPG 2026) in Guangzhou, Guangdong Province from September 18 to 20, 2026, and presented a poster for the paper "Wideband Oscillation Warning for Photovoltaic Power Stations Based on Online System-Equivalent-Impedance Assessment".

Addressing the difficulty of warning wideband oscillation risks in photovoltaic power stations in time and of identifying sensitive measurement points in advance, the paper proposes a wideband oscillation warning method based on online system-equivalent-impedance assessment: a small-signal impedance model of a single-unit grid-connected PV system is established, and warning and danger thresholds are determined according to the impedance ratio between the inverter and the grid. Fundamental phasors are extracted from synchronized voltage and current measurements at the transformer side, and the equivalent impedance of the external system at the port is identified online via the phasor difference between two moments; the equivalent inductance is then compared with the thresholds to achieve risk grading and sensitive-point screening. In a plant model composed of 32 PV units, different grid-strength conditions are set to verify the correspondence between the warning indicator and the subsequent oscillation response. Simulation results show that the sensitive ports screened out by the method exhibit the strongest oscillation response when the grid weakens further, providing a basis for wideband oscillation risk monitoring and operational intervention in PV power stations.
